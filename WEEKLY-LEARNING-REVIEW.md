# Weekly Learning Review Protocol

This review is a permanent part of the System Design learning process. It applies at the end of every week, after the weekly assessment and practical deliverable.

## Purpose

Measure learning quality, not just lesson completion. The goal is to build independent system-design reasoning and engineering judgement across the 24-week roadmap.

## Review dimensions

Score each dimension from 1–10, with evidence from the learner's explanations, exercises, design decisions, and assessment:

1. **Conceptual understanding** — Can the learner explain what a concept means and why it matters, in their own words?
2. **Problem-solving and reasoning** — Can the learner break down a problem, state assumptions, compare options, and justify decisions?
3. **Practical application** — Can the learner apply the concepts to the week's design or implementation deliverable?
4. **Retention** — Can the learner recall and connect earlier concepts without being retaught?
5. **Consistency and pace** — Is progress steady and sustainable, without rushing or overload?

Do not inflate scores for motivation. Be candid, constructive, and specific. Use examples from the learner's actual answers and work. Distinguish a knowledge gap from a one-off mistake or a communication/notation slip.

## Required weekly report

At the end of each week, provide:

- A scorecard with the five ratings and a short evidence-based explanation.
- What went well, with concrete examples.
- Gaps or weak areas, without sugarcoating.
- A focused revision plan; avoid repeating the whole week when a small targeted review is enough.
- Readiness for the next week.
- An overall judgement on whether the learner is on track toward the long-term HLD/LLD goals.

## Readiness outcomes

- **READY** — Foundations are sufficiently strong; proceed normally.
- **READY WITH REVISION** — Proceed while carrying one or two targeted revision tasks.
- **HOLD** — A key prerequisite is not understood; strengthen it before moving on.

Use the outcome that best reflects the evidence. Do not hold the learner for minor mistakes, but do not advance past a critical foundational gap.

## Timing and evidence

- Conduct the review only after the week's lessons, assessment, and deliverable have been completed.
- Use the full week's evidence, not only quiz scores.
- Record the summary in `PROGRESS.md` under the weekly review log and link any detailed review document if one is created.
- Update the week status only when its deliverable is genuinely complete.

## Teaching approach

Keep learning conversation-first and balanced: explain concepts through dialogue and practical examples, then assign selected external material only when it reinforces the current topic. Avoid assigning entire courses or overloading the learner. Introduce unfamiliar terms with clear labels such as **Learn now**, **Know for now**, and **Coming later**.
